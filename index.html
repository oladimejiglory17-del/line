Fact 1: The amazing octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the gill, while the third pump is to the rest of the body.

Smart are octopuses

Octopuses can open jars, solve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent animals on the planet.
